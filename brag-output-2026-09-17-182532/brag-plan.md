# Brag Plan: JesSee

## What is this app?
JesSee is a native Mac app that turns a narrated, annotated screen walkthrough into an editable visual story and a reusable PDF.

## The angle
A screen recording is natural to make but painful to send, scrub, and hand to AI. JesSee keeps the easy act of showing and explaining, uses annotations as evidence signals, and removes the timeline from the finished handoff.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A giant 18:42 timeline lands beside the line: “A screen recording is easy to make. Awful to use.” The frame immediately establishes the problem without generic AI language.

## Key moments (the middle)

- The native menu-bar app enters with recording actions, the three-part capture guide, and recent walkthroughs.
- The native image review becomes the hero: eight candidate screenshots, the selected frame, and a real highlight make the product intelligence visible.
- The finished PDF and the native Library/editor resolve the flow into something that can be shared, reopened, and revised.

## Outro / punchline
“Show it once. Send the useful version.” The JesSee icon and jessee.ai settle beneath it.

## User flow worth showing
Record and annotate a walkthrough → review the evidence and edit the story → export a PDF and return to it from the local Library.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: warm, editorial Mac product film with real screenshots and restrained physical motion
- Interpretation: clean feature-led pacing, large readable copy, confident holds, and smooth camera moves that let the real product carry the story.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22.4 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f7f5ef
- Accent: #5653e8
- Text: #18181b
- Display font: Manrope with system sans fallback
- Body font: Manrope with system sans fallback; Newsreader/Georgia for editorial emphasis
- Strongest visual element: the current native JesSee image review with ranked screenshots, markup controls, and the surrounding menu-bar app and Library/editor

## Share copy (draft)
JesSee turns one narrated, annotated walkthrough into an editable visual story and PDF—so your teammate or AI gets the useful context, not an 18-minute video to scrub.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents
- Music: none; the bundled music has unverified public redistribution terms and no authenticated catalog provider is available in this environment
- Music treatment: none
- Music cue guidance: the bundled 109.96 BPM cue grid informed visual timing only; no bundled track will ship
- Audio-reactive treatment: none because no music bed is used
- SFX posture: sparse, warm, CC0 interface clicks and soft impact cues matched to visible interactions
- Audio-coupled moments: cursor click on annotation, evidence selection, PDF/Library resolve, final logo landing
- Restraint rule: sound must support the product interaction and never make the film feel like a game or trailer
